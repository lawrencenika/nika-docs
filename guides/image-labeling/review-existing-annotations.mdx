Learn how to review annotations as a reviewer, provide feedback through comments, and approve or reject completed labeling work.

## Step 1: Add Comments During Review

A reviewer role can add comments with different severity levels for labelers to see in real time and reply.

![Add Comment on Image](/guides/image-labeling/review-existing-annotations-1.jpg)

To add a comment:
1. Click the comment button on the right
2. Click anywhere on the image to drop a comment there

Comment levels available:
- **INFO**: General information or suggestions
- **WARNING**: Issues that should be addressed
- **ERROR**: Critical problems that must be fixed

## Step 2: Manage Issues in the Right Panel

The labeler right panel displays a list of all issues (comments) added to the current image.

![Issues List in Right Panel](/guides/image-labeling/review-existing-annotations-2.jpg)

Users can:
- **Reply**: Other users can reply to issues for discussion
- **Resolve**: Mark an issue as resolved once addressed
- **Remove**: Issue owner can remove an issue entirely from the job

## Step 3: Filter and View Resolved Issues

Use the filter at the top right to control which issues are displayed.

![Filter Issues](/guides/image-labeling/review-existing-annotations-3.jpg)

The filter allows you to:
- **Show all issues**: View both open and resolved issues
- **View resolved issues**: See previously resolved issues
- **Reopen issues**: Reopen resolved issues for further actions if needed

## Step 4: Approve or Reject the Job

Once you've finished reviewing all images, you can approve or reject the job overall.

![Approve or Reject Job](/guides/image-labeling/review-existing-annotations-4.jpg)

As a reviewer:
1. Go back to the job overview page
2. Click **Reject** or **Approve** at the top right

This completes the review cycle and notifies the labeler of the outcome.

Happy labeling!
