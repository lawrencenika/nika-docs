## Answers You Can See and Share

NIKA doesn't just describe results — it builds them. As the agent works, it generates rich, interactive artifacts directly in the conversation, so the output of an analysis is something you can explore, present, and hand to a decision-maker, not a wall of text.

## From Analysis to Artifact

Every visualization is the end of an [autonomous analysis](/nika-desktop/autonomous-analysis): you ask in plain language, NIKA runs the work across your [connected data](/nika-desktop/data-and-conversion), and the result arrives as an artifact you can explore and share — no handoff to a separate GIS or BI tool required.
