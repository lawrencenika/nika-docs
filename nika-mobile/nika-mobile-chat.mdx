## Overview

NIKA Mobile's Agent Chat brings your team's GeoVault into the field. Ask questions in natural language and get AI-generated answers grounded in your project's documents, reports, and survey data — right from your phone.

## How It Works

Agent Chat connects to the same GeoVault that powers NIKA's desktop agent. When a field team member asks a question, the AI retrieves spatially-relevant knowledge from your team's ingested documents and synthesizes an answer with source citations.

### Example Queries

- *"What habitat types were recorded in the northern parcel last season?"*
- *"Are there any bat roost records within 500m of this site?"*
- *"What does our ESIA baseline say about the community livelihoods in this area?"*

## Key Features

### Spatial Context
Your GPS location is automatically included as context, so the AI prioritizes knowledge relevant to where you are standing. No need to specify coordinates or site names.

### Source Citations
Every answer references the original documents — PDFs, survey reports, or prior field data — so you can verify and trace back to primary sources.

### Team Knowledge
All team members share the same knowledge vault. Ingested documents, survey results, and reports contributed by any team member are available to everyone in the field.

### Offline Queued
When working in areas without connectivity, messages are queued locally and sent when you're back online. Previously cached responses remain accessible offline.
