## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Can I switch plans at any time?">
  Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll get immediate access to new features. When downgrading, changes take effect at the end of your billing cycle.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens after the Team Trial ends?">
  After 14 days, you can upgrade to the Team plan to keep your workspaces and data. If you don't upgrade, your account will revert to the Free plan and additional users will lose access.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do you offer discounts for academic or non-profit organizations?">
  Yes! We offer special pricing for academic institutions, non-profits, and government agencies. Contact us at [sales@nikaplanet.com](mailto:sales@nikaplanet.com) for details.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How does storage work?">
  Storage includes all uploaded datasets, generated outputs, and notebook files across your workspaces. You can monitor your usage from the <a href="https://planet.nika.eco/en/settings/usage" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">team usage dashboard</a> once logged in.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What compute resources are included?">
  Each plan includes different compute allocations for running notebooks and geospatial workloads:

  - **Free** — 21 GB RAM CPU (always available)
  - **Team Trial** — GPU T4x1 for limited hours, plus CPU
  - **Team** — Custom compute allocation based on your needs
  - **Enterprise** — Fully custom compute, including dedicated GPU options

  You can track your compute usage from the <a href="https://planet.nika.eco/en/settings/usage" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">team usage dashboard</a> once logged in.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
