## Overview

NikaField is an AI-first field survey application specifically built for environmental compliance. By integrating on-device AI and intelligent form processing, NikaField radically accelerates data collection in the field, moving beyond manual data entry into the era of Voice-to-Form AI.

![NikaField Survey List](/guides/field-survey/survey-list.png)

## Unmatched AI Capabilities

### Voice-to-Form AI Auto-Fill
Stop typing in the field. With NikaField's innovative Voice-to-Form AI, a surveyor can speak naturally about a habitat's condition, and our AI automatically extracts structured values into 15+ form fields. This provides a 10x productivity gain for field data entry.

![AI Auto-Fill](/guides/field-survey/survey-session-autofilled-from-transcript.png)

### On-Device Speech-to-Text
Powered by local, on-device transcription models. This means you can record audio and get transcriptions completely offline, which is critical in remote field sites without connectivity.

![Audio Transcript](/guides/field-survey/survey-session-audio%20mode-transcript.png)

### LLM-Powered Form Generation
Describe what you need in natural language, and NikaField automatically generates XLSForm schemas for you. There is no need to manually figure out spreadsheets or complex form logic to get started.

## Built for Environmental Compliance

### Compliance-Aware Schemas
NikaField includes pre-built templates customized to demanding environmental methodologies, including:
- Biodiversity Net Gain (BNG) 
- VM0033 Carbon Plots
- ESIA Baseline forms
- Bat and wildlife surveys

### Advanced Survey Hierarchy
Go beyond simple flat forms. NikaField supports rich parent-child relationships, repeat groups, and complex plot structures needed for comprehensive ecological audits.

![Survey Session](/guides/field-survey/survey-session.png)

### Built-in Mapping & GPS Trail
Navigate exactly where you need to be. With integrated GPS tracking, you can confidently review your survey paths, ensure complete coverage, and log accurate locations for plot stakes and transects.

![GPS Trail](/guides/field-survey/survey-session-trail.png)
